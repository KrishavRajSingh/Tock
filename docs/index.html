Tock Source code

Your Mac goes tock.

A small sound on every click. On scroll and key press too, if you want. 28 sounds to pick from, from desk taps to mechanical keyboards.

Download for Mac

Free and open source. macOS 13 or later. Under 1 MB.

Press a key to hear it. Hold, then let go: the release has its own sound.

Install

  1. Download Tock.dmg and open it.
  2. Drag Tock onto the Applications folder.
  3. Open Tock from Applications. macOS refuses the first time, because Tock isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate. Choose Done.
  4. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway. You only do this once.
  5. Pick a sound in the window that opens. Close it and Tock keeps running in the menu bar.

What Tock listens to

Mouse clicks and scrolling
No permission needed. Tock knows a button went down or the wheel moved, not what you clicked on.
Key presses, only if you turn them on
macOS asks you to allow Input Monitoring. Tock notices that a key went down or up. It never reads which key. After an update macOS may ask again.
Nothing leaves your Mac
Tock has no network code. The sounds are made on your Mac, from a few lines of maths each.
